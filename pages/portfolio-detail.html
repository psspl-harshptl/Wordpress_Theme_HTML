Document Management Solution

Business Case
Record of past clients and their confidential documents are always crucial aspects for lawyers. A law firm with a set of clients was in need to better manage their confidential documents…KNOW MORE

Technical Objectives

  • Craft & develop a portal for the new system
  • Organize the documents category and sub-category wise
  • Multi-file upload to document library
  • Documents report
  • Dashboard implementation
  • Master page branding

PSSPL Solution

  • SharePoint Online has been chosen as a platform to design and develop the new system. There can be several entities under one system; it can be a user or an organization.
  • Admin can create several numbers of entities (i.e. user or organization) and can save their details and other confidential documents within the system. Documents can be managed category wise, sub-category wise, etc.
  • A user can send emails with the confidential documents link.
  • Certain amount of meta-data is attached with each document and reports are provided to the certain role of users for the same. Document reports can be generated for thousands of records. The meta-data visibility of the report is based on the role of the user.
  • Export functionality is also provided under reports.

Application required Organizing the documents in category and sub category and upload Multiple files to document library.

Benefits

  • Reduced cost of operations.
  • Reduce Paper work.
  • Secure system.
