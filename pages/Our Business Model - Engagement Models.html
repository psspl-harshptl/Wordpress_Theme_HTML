Engagement Models

With Prakash Software, our customers have different options to select the most appropriate model to engage with us like As our customers have diverse needs along with their own way/process of executing project assignments, it becomes inevitable to offer multiple options to our customer to engage with us. This convenience allows our customers to choose or combine multiple models during the project execution lifecycle. Flexibility to adapt and align towards successful project execution during the engagement is one such value which we are able to offer.

Time & Material/Resource based

  • Hourly rate applicable based on the type of resource engaged
  • Want to keep all the control (Project management, especially)
  • Augment your own team with additional resources
  • Project requirements are not fixed (evolving in nature)

Dedicated Team

  • Want to set up a team of experts on specific technology
  • With an aim to augment to their own team in no time
  • For technology which they don’t have in-house capability

Fixed Cost / Project based

  • Requirements are clear and documented
  • Experience in working with Offshore delivery team on fixed cost project model.
