Document Management Portal

Overview

Project Summary

Built a centralized Document Management Portal on SharePoint Online with metadata-driven organization, versioning, and robust permissions to streamline enterprise-wide collaboration.

Key Features
  • Metadata & Retention
  • Approval Workflows
  • Search & Filters
  • Version Control
Business Outcomes
  • 40% faster retrieval
  • Better compliance
  • Reduced email loops
  • Higher adoption

Solution Details

Implemented custom site templates, content types, Power Automate flows for approvals, and role-based permissions integrated with Microsoft 365 groups. Provided migration support and adoption training.

Architecture

Hub site with departmental sites, managed metadata, and modern libraries.
